## Domain Vocabulary

**PIT**: Point-in-Time, expanding window (no future leakage).
**regime posterior**: P(state_t | obs[1:t]), three or eight discrete market states.
**ship-gate**: decision layer gating alpha promotion (regime, PBO, control).
**state_tag**: regime label (bear / neutral / bull / risk_on / risk_off).
**state_argmax**: hard regime assignment (arg max of posterior).
**refit_every**: EM refitting cadence in trading days (default 21 = monthly).
**era**: refit epoch [era_start, era_end); steady params in between.
**forward filter**: incremental posterior update using fixed transition/emission (CPU O(T), GPU parallel (B,K)).
**log1p**: log(1 + x) transform for heavy-tail features (Amihud illiquidity proxy).
**DenseHMM**: pomegranate multi-state HMM (vs single-state Gaussian in hmmlearn).
